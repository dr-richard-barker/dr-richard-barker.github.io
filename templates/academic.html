Scholarly Profile & Research Program

Interdisciplinary Systems Biology

I am a plant systems biologist, biophysical instrumentation developer, and computational researcher specializing in photosynthetic boundary-layer transport, primary carbon allocation, organelle stress signaling, and biological digital twins. Trained in hormone biochemistry and genetics at Rothamsted Research and the University of Nottingham (Lawes Trust Agricultural Scholar) under Prof. Peter Hedden, I spent eleven years in research and instructional leadership at the University of Wisconsin–Madison across the Departments of Botany and Genetics.

My program bridges biophysical experimentation with high-throughput computational biology. I serve as Science Co-Lead for the NASA Lunar LEAF Science Definition Team (with Prof. D. Marshall Porterfield at Purdue University), direct the international NASA GeneLab Plant Analysis Working Group (AWG) representing 100+ members, and maintain over 150 open-source repositories of FAIR computational pipelines, IoT phenotyping firmware, and biological modeling suites. I have co-secured ~$2,000,000 in competitive extramural funding and received the 2018 UW–Madison Undergraduate Mentoring Award.
